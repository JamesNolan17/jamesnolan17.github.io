Selected Publications (First Author)

  • Fail-Fast, Restart-Smart: Early Failure Prediction and Restart for SWE Agentic Tasks — under review, 2027 (arXiv:2608.03222)
    • A 0.6B monitor supervises 27B+ coding agents at inference — 14.6–20.4% execution-token savings at a 5% false-positive rate; SWE-bench Verified issue resolution 66.6% → 71.8%.
    • One monitor transfers across four agents, including a closed-API model.
  • Backdoors in Code Summarizers: How Bad Is It? — ASE 2025
    • 20 of 454,451 samples (0.004%) implant a backdoor that the standard spectral-signature defense fails to remove — far below rates previously assumed safe.
  • Industry Practitioners’ Perspectives on AI Model Quality — EASE 2026 (invited to IEEE Software)
    • 15 interviews and a 50-practitioner survey on how practitioners prioritize AI model quality; e.g., for real-time AI, efficiency is a hard constraint over correctness.
